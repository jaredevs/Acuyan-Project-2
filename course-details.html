Bachelor of Science in Business Administration

The BSBA program prepares students for careers in business and management. Students develop competencies in financial analysis, marketing strategy, organizational management, and entrepreneurship.

KCC's program combines practical business learning with ethical leadership, service, and whole-person formation.

What You'll Learn

  • Financial and management analysis
  • Marketing strategy and research
  • Organizational leadership
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Business communication
  • Operations and resource management
  • Responsible decision-making
  • Community-oriented service

Management and Leadership

Understand how organizations plan, operate, and serve their communities.

The management component develops analytical thinking, leadership, human resource awareness, operations management, and ethical decision-making.

Accountancy and Finance

Build a strong foundation in responsible financial management.

Students learn financial analysis, accounting principles, budgeting, taxation concepts, and transparent reporting for organizations.

Technology and Information Systems

Use digital tools to support modern business operations.

Business students develop awareness of databases, digital communication, information systems, and technology-supported decision-making.

Tourism and Hospitality

Explore service and destination management in a dynamic industry.

The CMAT college also offers tourism-focused studies in planning, event management, sustainable tourism, and visitor service.

Career and Community Outcomes

Prepare for professional work and meaningful service.

Graduates may pursue careers in management, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship, operations, and public or private organizations while carrying KCC's values of faith and service.
